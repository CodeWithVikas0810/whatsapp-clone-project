# User Personas

## Persona 1: Vikas Mehta

### Background

* Age: 25
* Occupation: Software Engineer
* Location: Bangalore, India

### Goals

* Communicate with friends and colleagues through instant messaging.
* Share text, images, documents, and voice messages easily.
* Stay connected with personal and professional contacts.
* Create and participate in group conversations.
* Share daily updates through status.
* Make audio and video calls with contacts.

### Needs

* A fast and reliable one-to-one messaging experience.
* Real-time message delivery and read receipts.
* Ability to share different types of media and files.
* Group chats with member and admin management.
* Simple profile and privacy settings.
* Audio and video calling functionality.

### Pain Points

* Switching between different applications for messages, calls, and media sharing.
* Delayed message delivery or unreliable communication.
* Difficulty managing conversations across multiple groups and contacts.
* Limited visibility into whether messages have been delivered or read.

## Persona 2: Priya Mehta

### Background

* Age: 22
* Occupation: College Student
* Location: Uttarakhand, India

### Goals

* Stay connected with friends and family.
* Quickly communicate through text and voice messages.
* Share photos, videos, and daily updates.
* Participate in college and friend groups.
* Keep track of important conversations and shared media.
* Make audio and video calls without switching applications.

### Needs

* An easy-to-use chat interface.
* Real-time notifications for new messages.
* Ability to reply to, react to, and forward messages.
* Simple group creation and member management.
* Status updates for sharing photos, videos, and text.
* Reliable media and document sharing.

### Pain Points

* Losing track of important messages in active group chats.
* Difficulty finding specific conversations or shared content.
* Managing multiple communication apps for different purposes.
* Missing important messages when notifications are delayed.
